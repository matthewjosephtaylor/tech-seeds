## Planting

Give a seed directory to your coding AI and ask it to build. Its README supplies the goal, owner choices and delivery criteria; the technical specification supplies defaults, records, algorithms and examples. Ordinary implementation decisions belong to the AI; sources, spending, recurring work and exposure belong to you. Your environment's permissions still govern.

Seeds are dated, curated specifications, not installers or autonomous authority. No telemetry, account with us or access to the originating installation is required. Implementations own their source/data and may evolve. Prove useful behavior through the actual application, not a checklist of promised features.

## Contributing another seed

Use `<name>/README.md` as the entrypoint. Include enough technical and creative material to build without private context; distinguish reference choices from essential behavior and unverified claims. Keep side effects auditable. Prefer a few dense files over a framework, registry or universal bootstrap script.
